# Production image sources

The material and terrain PNGs were generated with Codex's built-in `image_gen` tool for this project on 2026-09-18. They are opaque 4×4 material grids, retained at the generator's original resolution. The Rust tool filters the material sheet into per-face UV tiles, renders eight orthographic camera stencils and projects them back while preserving prior texels. Internal surfaces use the corresponding material tile as a completion step. Model silhouettes and animations are authored in `../catalog.mjs`, not generated by the image model.

## materials.png

Zero-based material indices, row-major:

| 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Oak planks | Tree bark | Deciduous leaves | Pine needles |
| 4: Olive steel | 5: Iron | 6: Pale steel | 7: Canvas |
| 8: Leather | 9: Skin | 10: Limestone | 11: Roof tile |
| 12: Solar cells | 13: Brass | 14: Straw | 15: Blue glass |

Exact generation prompt:

> Create a production game MATERIAL SOURCE ATLAS, square image, exactly 4 columns by 4 rows of equal square texture swatches, no gutters, no borders, no labels or text, all tiles filling their squares completely, flat orthographic close-up surface photographs under perfectly diffuse lighting, no perspective, no objects, no cast shadows. Cohesive realistic worn/grungy material textures for simple low-poly strategy-game assets. Row 1 left to right: (1) warm aged oak planks with grain and knots, (2) dark rough tree bark with vertical fissures, (3) dense olive green deciduous leaves viewed close-up as opaque canopy surface, (4) dark pine needles as opaque dense conifer canopy surface. Row 2: (5) scratched olive-drab painted steel, (6) weathered charcoal iron metal, (7) pale dull brushed steel, (8) faded tan canvas cloth weave. Row 3: (9) worn dark brown leather, (10) warm neutral tan skin surface subtle pores with NO facial features, (11) coarse grey limestone masonry blocks, (12) weathered brown-red clay roof tiles. Row 4: (13) dark blue solar photovoltaic panel cells, (14) aged brass metal, (15) yellowed dry wheat straw, (16) deep blue grey glass. Exactly uniform 4x4 grid, restrained realistic material variation, surfaces fully opaque. No simulated bevels, no sphere render previews, no text.

## terrain.png

Zero-based tile indices: soil 0, grass 1, forest floor 2, beach sand 3, rock 4, snow 5, ocean color 6, cloud 7, shallow water 8, ash 9, embers 10, paving 11, desert sand 12, moss 13, dirt 14, water height 15. Some swatches are reserved for future effects. The source becomes a 512px runtime atlas. Ocean normal and roughness maps derive from tile 15 at 128px. Fog-of-war is a gameplay mask and is not painted with an image generator.

The current terrain source replaces the initial noisy photographic sheet. See `terrain-direction.md` for the mini-Earth reference, extracted palette, exact replacement prompts, and mapping design.
